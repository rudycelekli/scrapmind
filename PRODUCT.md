# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Makers, curious inventors, and people repurposing spare devices and materials. They work beside actual objects at a desk or workshop bench and need to decide what to build, which parts to use, and what still needs testing. A public demo inventory lets newcomers explore before declaring their own items.

## Product Purpose

SCRAPMIND is an AI invention workbench with Device Alchemy integrated. Its primary workflow is inventory → AI ideas → guided builds → evidence. Success means a person can inspect an idea, understand its physical requirements, substitute an unavailable part, follow its procedure, and record evidence without losing provenance. The user confirmed this proposed direction on 2026-10-02 after the product interview and asked for a world-class, strongly AI-powered product.

## Brand Personality

Futuristic, tangible, precise. Evoke the excitement of discovering a new use for an ordinary object. Use confident, concise language grounded in the actual available parts. Make the workbench memorable and suitable for demonstration through real interactions, with no invented performance or research claims.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI chat layouts, decorative dashboards, fake telemetry, invented success metrics, repetitive icon cards, and cinematic renderings that imply an untested assembly works. The interface should make the physical workflow understandable rather than hide it behind a conversation.

## Design Principles

1. Begin with the objects a person has. Keep identity, availability, quantity, measurements, and evidence understandable.
2. Let AI propose and explain. Preserve reasoning, assumptions, draft review, model provenance, and unresolved resource issues.
3. Show the configuration. Connect requirements and instructions to actual allocated items and make substitutions visible.
4. Close the loop with evidence. Capture and checks belong to the current build revision, and stale evidence must remain visibly stale.
5. Earn the spectacle. Memorable moments come from useful replanning, device reuse, and inspectable results, not unsupported claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and explicit labels are baseline requirements. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA; do not claim conformance without auditing the implemented interface. Camera access remains an explicit user action. The app must remain useful when permission is denied, no camera exists, or no AI model is configured.
